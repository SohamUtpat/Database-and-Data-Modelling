Given the table:

1.orders _id, user_id, status_created_at 

2.Suggest appropriate indexes 

3.Explain why you chose them




Answers 


1. Appropriate Indexes -------

        1.Primary index on order_id

        2.Index on user_id

        3.Composite index on (user_id, created_at)

        4.Composite index on (status, created_at)


2. why choose them -----

     1. Primary Index on order_id ----- 

             1.Each order must be uniquely identifiable.

             2.Provides very fast access when searching by order ID.

             3.Ensures data integrity by preventing duplicate orders.

    
     2.Index on user_id -----

             1.Orders are often retrieved per user.

             2.Improves performance for queries that filter orders by a specific user.

             3.Helps optimize joins between the orders table and a users table.


     3.Composite Index on (user_id, created_at) -----

             1.Supports queries that fetch a user’s order history.

             2.Efficient when orders are displayed in chronological order.

             3.Reduces sorting overhead by combining filtering and ordering.


     4.Composite Index on (status, created_at) -----

             1.Useful for filtering orders based on their status (e.g., pending, shipped).

             2.Helps in generating reports and monitoring order progress.

             3.Optimizes time-based queries on order status.
